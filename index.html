About Ndlawtech

Creative thinking. Purposeful design.

I’m a freelance graphic designer working under the name Ndlawtech. I help businesses, entrepreneurs and growing brands present their products, services and ideas through clear, engaging visual content.

My services combine social media design and short-form video editing. I focus on clean layouts, strong visual hierarchy, readable messaging and content that fits each brand’s audience and platform.

My approach is simple: every design should look professional, communicate clearly and give the audience a reason to take action.
